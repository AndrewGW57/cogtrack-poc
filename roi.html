ROI model  ·  Built for the centre's own numbers

What paperwork costs today, and what a single patient record returns.

Enter your programs, your clinicians and your time. The model separates money you stop spending, capacity you can sell, and growth you might win. Each is labeled by how firm it is.

Nothing is pre-filled. Enter the centre's own figures. Results appear as soon as patients and fees are entered, and each section adds to them. Start with Section 1.
Section 1

Programs and patients

Patients per month and fee per patient for each service line. Leave a line blank if the centre does not run it.

Clinician paperwork per patient

Section 3

Coordination and the final report

Each clinician sends results to one repository. A master template builds the final report with index, page numbers, headers and footers. This section prices the administrative work that replaces.

Section 4

What CogTrack changes

These sliders decide how much of the time saved becomes money. Saved minutes are worth nothing until they cut paid hours or fill a bookable slot.

Section 5 Speculative

Growth

Follow-up testing and new contracts. Treat these as upside to test in a pilot, not as a promise.

Section 6

Cost to the centre

Leave both at zero for a barter pilot. Enter figures to see net benefit and payback.

How to read this. Cost avoided is firm only where hours actually leave payroll, overtime or locum spend. Capacity revenue is conditional on demand: bookable slots need patients. Growth is unproven. Revenue is shown after a contribution margin, because extra patients carry their own costs. A pilot replaces estimates with measured data.
